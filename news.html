More popular games are now featured in the GameZone library.

Upcoming GameZone Events

Check out the latest community and gaming events.

October 2026

Competitive Gaming Community Grows

More players are joining the GameZone competitive gaming community.

Competitive gaming is not only about winning; it is also a way for players to meet others who enjoy the same games. GameZone's Community page provides General Chat for open discussions and Find Players for people who want to talk about teammates and playing together. These features give visitors a place to share gaming-related messages.

Visitors can enter a username and share a message in the available chat area. Players can discuss their favorite games, ask questions, and look for people with similar interests. For this prototype, community messages are saved in the current browser's local storage. This demonstrates the chat interface, but it is not a shared live chat service between different users or devices.

GameZone Website Improvements

New features and interface improvements continue to be added to GameZone.

The GameZone interface has been updated with a consistent dark theme, game artwork, clearer cards, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes. These visual changes help the pages feel connected while making important information, such as game genres, news categories, and tournament dates, easier to scan.

The site also brings several interactive features together: game search and genre filters, news category filtering, expandable article details, player login and registration, tournament registration, a contact form, and community chat. Together, these features demonstrate how HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and browser storage work together to create a website with multiple connected pages and interactive functions.

October 2026

Tournament Registration Now Available

Players can register for selected GameZone tournaments.

The tournament registration form lets players select one of the listed GameZone events and provide the information needed for the demonstration, including gamer name, email address, tournament choice, and gaming experience. Required fields help prevent incomplete submissions, and a confirmation message appears after a valid submission so visitors know the form was processed.

Before submitting, check the tournament name and date on its card and make sure your details are correct. Players should also review the selected event before completing the form. In this project version, registration records are stored in the browser's local storage rather than a central online database, so they are intended for testing and presentation rather than official tournament entry.
